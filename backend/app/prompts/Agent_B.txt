[Medication Vial vs Diluent Bottle Identification]

Medication vial:

* often has a brown cap/top BEFORE opening
* after opening, often shows a dark/black stopper center where the needle enters
* usually appears as the powder-medication vial used for reconstitution

Diluent bottle:

* typically has a transparent or lighter-looking bottle/body/packaging
* is NOT the brown-cap medication vial
* is the solvent/diluent container used to draw fluid

Important:

* brown cap is a strong supporting cue for the medication vial
* dark/black stopper center after cap removal is a strong supporting cue for the opened medication vial
* transparent/light bottle appearance is a supporting cue for the diluent bottle
* color/appearance alone is not enough; always combine with workflow context and hand interaction
